### Historical / neighboring geometry bridge

Athanase Papadopoulos's work is not being identified with Buliga's dilation structures. A small subset is useful here because it tracks older routes into projective/Finsler metrics, non-Euclidean geometry, quasiconformal distortion, and local geometric comparison.

See [`papadopoulos-geometry-bridges.md`](papadopoulos-geometry-bridges.md).

## People and prior work that recur in this line

The sources above repeatedly situate the work relative to, among others:

- Mikhail Gromov — metric/sub-Riemannian geometry and tangent limits;
- André Bellaïche — tangent-space constructions in sub-Riemannian geometry;
- Pierre Pansu — differential calculus and Carnot-group geometry;
- Eberhard Siebert — contractive automorphisms / homogeneous groups;
- Roger Fenn, Colin Rourke, David Joyce — racks and quandles;
- Ott-Heinrich Keller/Loos tradition on symmetric spaces (via Buliga's references);
- Louis H. Kauffman — knot/logic connections and chemlambda collaboration;
- Yves Lafont — interaction nets/combinators;
- Alan Bawden — connection/port-graph ideas cited in the rewrite work.

When expanding these notes, credit the specific source being used rather than converting this list into a generic acknowledgement.
